What to expect

Every seminar runs about 60–90 minutes and includes time for questions. We keep group sizes manageable so everyone gets attention, and we're always happy to slow down or repeat something. Feel free to bring your own phone, tablet, or laptop if you'd like hands-on help.

Heads up: our seminars are educational only. We won't ask you to install software, share passwords, or provide payment information — and you should be cautious of anyone else who does.

Bring a seminar to your group

We regularly visit libraries, senior centers, churches, and community organizations across Kitsap County. If you'd like to host a seminar for your group, let us know a bit about your organization and preferred dates.
