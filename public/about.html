About Peninsula Cyber

We're neighbors helping neighbors stay safe online.

Our story

Peninsula Cyber started with a simple observation: the people most targeted by online scams — seniors and families — are often the least served by the security industry. Most cybersecurity resources are written for IT professionals, full of jargon that leaves everyday people more confused, not less.

So we set out to do something different. We hold free seminars in libraries, senior centers, and community spaces across Kitsap County, teaching practical, judgment-free security skills in plain language. No slideshows full of acronyms. No scare tactics. Just clear explanations and time for your questions.

What we believe

Security isn't scary when you understand it. We believe everyone deserves to feel confident using technology, regardless of age or experience. We focus on empowering people with the knowledge to protect themselves, rather than making them feel foolish for not already knowing it.

A note on trust: Peninsula Cyber never asks for payment, passwords, or remote access to your devices during a seminar or follow-up. If someone claiming to be from Peninsula Cyber asks for these things, it isn't us — please contact us to report it.

Rooted in the Pacific Northwest

We're based right here on the Kitsap Peninsula, and our seminars go where our community already gathers. If you'd like us to bring a seminar to your library, senior center, church, or community group, we'd love to hear from you.
