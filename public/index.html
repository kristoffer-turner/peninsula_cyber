Our mission

Making cybersecurity attainable for our community

Scams and online threats affect everyone, but they hit seniors and families especially hard. Peninsula Cyber brings clear, judgment-free security education straight to Kitsap County — at libraries, senior centers, and community spaces you already know.

Free Seminars

Every seminar is free and open to the public, taught in plain language with plenty of time for questions.

Practical Tips

We focus on habits you can start using the same day — spotting scams, securing accounts, and protecting your family.

Community Focus

We're your neighbors, not a vendor. Our seminars are grounded in the towns and people of the Kitsap Peninsula.
